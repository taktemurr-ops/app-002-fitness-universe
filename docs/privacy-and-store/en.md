# Fitness Universe — English store and privacy draft

**Short description:** Offline workout journal with multilingual tracking.

**Description:** Log walking, running, cycling, strength training and yoga. Track durations, view workout history and remove entries. English, Turkish and Arabic interface resources are included.

## Data handling — current MVP
- Workout type, duration and timestamps are saved locally in shared_preferences, without encryption.
- No login, analytics, advertising SDK, cloud synchronization or network transmission is implemented.
- Individual workout deletion is available. Full data export and delete-all controls are not yet implemented.
- This is a workout log, not a clinical assessment or medical advice.

## Release blockers
Review RTL and accessible interaction on devices; verify locale-specific numbers, store disclosures and backup policy; implement full erasure/export and assess secure storage before launch. This draft is **not** a final legal privacy policy.
